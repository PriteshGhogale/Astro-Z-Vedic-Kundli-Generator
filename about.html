About Astro-Z Vedic Kundli

Astro-Z Vedic Kundli is a free, browser-based tool for generating a Vedic (Jyotish) astrology birth chart — a "kundli" — from a person's birth date, time, and place. Everything runs client-side: no account, no sign-up, and your birth details never leave your device except for an optional place-name lookup.

What is a Kundli?

In Vedic astrology, a kundli is a map of the sky — specifically the positions of the Sun, Moon, and planets along the zodiac — at the exact moment and location of a person's birth. Practitioners use this chart to study personality traits, life themes, and timing of events through planetary periods called Dashas.

What this tool calculates

  • Sidereal Rasi (D1) birth chart, in both North Indian and South Indian visual styles
  • Navamsa (D9) chart, used for marriage and refined planetary strength
  • Ascendant (Lagna), Moon sign, and birth Nakshatra (lunar mansion)
  • Planetary status: retrograde, combust, exalted, debilitated, own sign, and friendly/enemy sign placements
  • Panchang details at birth: Tithi, Nitya Yoga, Karana, and weekday
  • Vimshottari Mahadasha and Antardasha planetary period tables
  • A downloadable PDF report combining all of the above

How accurate is it?

Planetary positions are calculated using simplified mean-orbit astronomical formulas with the Lahiri ayanamsa, rather than a full ephemeris. This is accurate to roughly sign and nakshatra level for most dates — reliable enough for exploration and general reading, but not intended to replace professional-grade astronomical software for applications requiring second-by-second precision.

FAQ

Is my birth information saved anywhere?
No. All calculations happen in your browser. Nothing is sent to or stored on any server we control.
What's the difference between North Indian and South Indian chart styles?
Both show identical planetary placements — they're just two regional visual conventions. North Indian charts use fixed house positions with signs rotating based on the ascendant; South Indian charts use fixed sign positions with houses rotating instead.
What is a Dasha?
A Dasha is a planetary time period used in Vedic astrology to time life events. The Vimshottari Dasha system — the most widely used — divides a 120-year cycle among nine planets based on the Moon's position at birth.
Can I trust this for major life decisions?
This tool is intended for educational and exploratory purposes. For decisions where accuracy genuinely matters to you, consult a qualified astrologer using professional-grade ephemeris software.
