Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Astro-Z Vedic Kundli ("we," "this site," "the app") is a free tool for generating Vedic astrology birth charts. This page explains what information is involved when you use it.

Birth details you enter

The name, date, time, and place of birth you enter are used only to run calculations directly in your browser. This data is not sent to, stored on, or logged by any server we operate. If you close or refresh the page, the information is gone. The PDF report you can download is generated entirely on your device.

Birth place lookup (geocoding)

If you use the "Find" button to look up coordinates for a place name, that place name is sent to OpenStreetMap's free Nominatim geocoding service to retrieve latitude and longitude. This request goes directly from your browser to OpenStreetMap — we do not see or store it. See Nominatim's usage policy for details on how they handle requests.

Analytics

We may use standard, privacy-respecting analytics (such as aggregate visitor counts) to understand site traffic. No birth chart data is ever included in analytics.

Children's privacy

This site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this page from time to time. Continued use of the site after changes means you accept the updated policy.
